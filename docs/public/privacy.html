Privacy Policy

AI Running Coach · Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Who operates this application

AI Running Coach is a personal, non-commercial project operated by an individual developer. It is not a company, not a commercial service, and is not offered for sale.

Contact for all privacy matters: iamwujianhua@163.com

2. What data is collected

Data is accessed only after you explicitly authorize it through OAuth, and only for the scopes you approve:

  • Oura Ring — sleep score, sleep duration, readiness score, night HRV, lowest and average heart rate, body temperature deviation, workouts
  • Garmin — recovery metrics (Body Battery, stress, resting heart rate), activities, GPS tracks
  • Strava — activities, distance, pace, heart rate, cadence, elevation
  • Your own inputs — training check-ins, shoes, notes

No account password is stored for OAuth sources. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using Fernet symmetric encryption.

3. How the data is used

Data is used for exactly one purpose: generating personalized training and recovery recommendations that are displayed to you.

This includes computing training load (CTL / ATL / TSB), acute-to-chronic workload ratio, weekly summaries, and pacing guidance.

4. Where data is stored

All data is stored in a self-hosted SQLite database on a private server controlled by the operator. There is no cloud data warehouse, no analytics platform, and no advertising SDK.

The server is protected by an access token; all API requests require it.

5. Sharing with third parties

To generate coaching text, a summary of your recent training and recovery data is sent to a large language model provider. The provider is chosen by you in the app settings and may be DeepSeek, OpenAI, or Doubao (Volcengine).

What is sent: recent recovery values, recent activity summaries, and your question. What is not sent: your name, email, OAuth tokens, GPS coordinates, or raw time-series samples.

We do not sell, rent, license, or share your data for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not disclose data to any other third party.

6. How long data is kept

Data is retained for as long as the data source remains connected, so that long-term trends can be computed. You may delete it at any time — see below.

7. Your rights and controls

  • Disconnect — in the app, open Data Sources and tap Disconnect on any source. This revokes further collection immediately.
  • Delete — email the address above to have all stored data erased. Requests are honored within 30 days.
  • Revoke at the provider — you can also revoke this app's access from your Oura account settings at any time.
  • Export — you may request a copy of your stored data by email.

8. Security

OAuth tokens and API keys are encrypted at rest. The server requires a shared access token for all API calls. Transport uses HTTPS for OAuth callbacks and TLS where supported.

This is a single-user personal project, not an audited enterprise system. No security guarantee is made beyond reasonable care.

9. Children

This application is not directed at anyone under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the application changes. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
